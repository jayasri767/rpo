About GMRIT

GMR Institute of Technology (GMRIT) is an autonomous institution affiliated to JNTU Kakinada, located in Rajam, Andhra Pradesh. Established by the GMR Varalakshmi Foundation under the visionary leadership of Sri G. M. Rao, the institute has been providing quality technical education since 1997.

The institute is spread over a sprawling 100-acre campus with state-of-the-art infrastructure, well-equipped laboratories, modern classrooms, and excellent hostel facilities. GMRIT is committed to nurturing technically competent, socially responsible, and environmentally conscious engineers.

With a strong emphasis on research and innovation, GMRIT encourages students to participate in various technical competitions, research projects, and industry collaborations to enhance their practical knowledge and skills.
